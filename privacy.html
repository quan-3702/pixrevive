PixRevive privacy policy

PixRevive works entirely on your own computer. Your photos are never uploaded, collected or shared. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

The first time you use an AI tool, the AI model (about 30–55 MB) is downloaded once from Hugging Face and stored on your PC so the app works offline afterwards. No photo or personal information is sent with that download.

Purchases of PixRevive Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.
