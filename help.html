PixRevive – How to use

Make old, small and blurry photos look new – with AI that runs on your own PC.

1. Open a photo2. Sharpen and enlarge3. Erase objects and scratches4. Bring back colour5. Compare, undo and save6. Free version and Pro7. If something goes wrong

1. Open a photo

Click 📂 Open photo, drop a picture onto the window, or paste one with Ctrl+V. For an old paper photo, scan it or take a straight, well-lit picture of it with your phone.

2. Sharpen and enlarge

  1. Choose 2× bigger or 4× bigger.
  2. Click ✨ Enhance photo.

The AI adds real detail – sharper edges, clearer faces and text – instead of only stretching the pixels. The first time, the AI is downloaded once (about 55 MB); after that it works offline.

A big photo takes longer – from a few seconds to a few minutes, depending on your PC. Enhance first, then erase and fix colours.

3. Erase objects and scratches

  1. Paint over what you want to remove – a person in the background, a wire, a scratch, a date stamp or a logo. Change the Brush size for small or big things.
  2. Click 🧽 Erase. The painted area is filled in to match the background.
  3. Not perfect? Paint again over what is left and erase once more, or press Undo.

The first time, the eraser AI is downloaded once (about 28 MB).

4. Bring back colour

  • 🎨 Auto colour fixes faded, yellow or dull photos in one click.
  • Warmth makes the photo warmer (more yellow) or cooler (more blue).
  • ⚫ Black & white turns the photo into a classic black-and-white picture.

5. Compare, undo and save

  • Tick Compare with the original and move the mouse over the photo to slide between before and after.
  • ↶ Undo (Ctrl+Z) and ↷ Redo (Ctrl+Y) step back and forward.
  • 💾 Save (Ctrl+S) saves a PNG in your Downloads folder. ☰ More → Save as JPG makes a smaller file to e-mail or share.

6. Free version and Pro

Erasing, colour fixes and saving are free and never add a watermark. The free version enlarges photos 2× up to 1 megapixel. Pro is a one-time purchase – no subscription – and adds 4× enlarging and photos of any size.

7. If something goes wrong

  • “The AI model could not be downloaded”: connect to the internet once and try again. After the first time PixRevive works offline.
  • Enhancing is slow: close other programs, or enlarge 2× instead of 4×.
  • Erasing left a smudge: paint a little wider than the object, including its shadow, and erase again.
